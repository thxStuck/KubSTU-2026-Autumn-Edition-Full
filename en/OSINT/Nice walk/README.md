## 📌 Task

Once I was strolling through a beautiful southern city and noticed this beautiful statue, but unfortunately I forgot the name of this goddess. Help me remember where I saw her and what her name is.

## 🏁 Solution

### Step 1. Analyzing the photo

The statue:
A white statue of a girl (or goddess) in the antique style. In her hand she holds an object resembling a bowl or a lamp.

The surroundings:
In the background you can see a modern 2–3 storey building. Next to the statue there is a neat flower bed with silvery-white plants — typical decorative plantings often found in parks.

Nature:
The weather is sunny. The leaves on the trees are in yellow and green shades, which points to the time of year — the beginning or the middle of autumn.

### Step 2. First conclusions

Based on these details, we can confidently assume that the photo was taken in a park or on an alley located near sanatoriums, hotels or a resort zone.

### Step 3. Narrowing the geography

The architecture and the layout of the area hint at a southern region. However, the photo lacks palm trees and other subtropical plants that are usually found on seaside promenades and resorts. This lets us rule out coastal cities and focus on southern cities inland.

### Step 4. Checking via external resources

We upload the photo to the "Search by image" service on google.com.

![google_search.png](../../../assets/OSINT/Nice%20walk/google_search.png)

Among the results several similar images appear, by which we manage to determine that the photo shows a statue of the ancient Greek goddess Hebe.

### Step 5. The final search

We search on maps for "Girl with a bowl, southern city" / "Goddess Hebe, southern city". The service returns a monument located in the city of Goryachy Klyuch. This is the location we are looking for.
